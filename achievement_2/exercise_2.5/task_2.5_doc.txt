Frontend inspirations:

https://blog.prototypr.io/the-tasty-app-case-study-dbd0f38065e7
I found an article about the development of the Tasty
app, which is used for finding recipes and watching
videos on how to make them. In their final frontend
build version, their layout is well-executed and
simplistic, while not looking too barebones. The colors
used are consistent and appropriate to the brand, and
spacing between elements is well-executed.

https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.tudorspan.recipekeeper&hl=en_US
Similarly to Tasty, Recipe Keeper has a layout that makes
the most use of screen space. It also has a wide range of
categories and options for searching recipes. This app is
a good contrast to Tasty as the latter makes good use of
negative space to avoid appearing cluttered. That being
said, Recipe Keeper does a good job of balancing out the
space it has to work with and making the most of it.
